CANONICAL CUSTOMER REPORT:
Use report.schemaVersion="3", scope="SELECTED_PASSAGES". All lists are arrays, including when empty. Keep legacy issues=[], bulletRewrites=[], missingInformation=[], fixThisFirst=[], and actionPlan={quickWins:[],mediumEffort:[],deepFixes:[]}. Advice belongs ONLY in report, not repeated in those legacy fields.

report.priorities: up to three distinct high-value recommendations with title,reason,editIds. Do not force three if fewer are useful. A major qualification gap without an editable solution can have editIds=[] and must remain visible in outcomes.

report.edits: select the passages with the most material value. No minimum rewrite quota. Each has unique stable id, operation (replace|remove|retain|add|move|merge), section (employer/project and passage), before, after, insertAfter, reason, sourceQuotes, requirementIds.
- before must be an exact source passage from the supplied readable source, with the same case, words, numbers and punctuation; it must identify exactly one source location. sourceQuotes must be exact source excerpts that uniquely identify their locations and support EVERY factual clause. Include employer context in the excerpt where needed. A short ambiguous fragment is not sufficient evidence.
- after is finished truthful wording, with NO brackets, placeholders, TO-FILL, invented metrics, or unknowns. Preserve useful nonnumeric outcomes explicitly reported in the source. Architecture decisions, correctness, recovery, and technical influence can be evidence without numbers. When more detail would help, deliver a useful version now and ask separately.
- replace rewrites one original passage; remove has after=""; retain has after identical to before. add has before="" and requires an exact unique original insertAfter anchor. move removes before and inserts after following the exact original insertAfter anchor. merge replaces ONE CONTIGUOUS original span in before; never combine noncontiguous source bullets through an ambiguous before string. insertAfter="" for replace/remove/retain/merge. Avoid overlapping changed spans or insertion anchors within spans being changed.
- requirementIds references actual topPriorities IDs; [] for non-job-specific cleanup. Preserve ownership verbs and numbers from supporting evidence. Do not make cosmetic changes to inflate the amount of work. A correct strong passage can simply remain unchanged.

report.sections: an ordered selection of revised summary/experience/project passages. Include strong retained evidence from the material roles so the revised-report view remains useful without rewriting already strong bullets. Do not force cosmetic edits or add a quota. The application derives passage order from source locations and insertion anchors; do not generate source offsets or items arrays. Each has heading,editIds,retained. Reference every non-removed edit exactly once. Do NOT reference removed edits. retained is a list of {text,sourceQuote}, with text IDENTICAL to the exact original sourceQuote. Never independently rewrite passages in this field; edited wording exists only in report.edits. Preserve chronological employer ordering and contractor relationships; a source-backed summary may use supported facts across roles without moving them into another employer's experience. Do not imply selected passages are a full replacement resume.

report.questions: a short ordered queue of specific questions grounded in plausible prior work and this job. Each question asks for ONE manageable answer about ONE competency or example. Maximum 45 words and 260 characters, one question mark and no semicolons. Do not combine multiple tools, employment dates, measurements, implementation scope and trade-offs into one question. Ask the single most valuable question first; place brief optional context in why rather than adding more questions to question. For example: "When did you first use Terraform professionally?" or "What did you personally implement in one recent backend component?" The UI displays one question initially; each has id,question,why,editIds. Prioritize questions that could materially change an edited passage or unresolved requirement. Questions may have editIds=[] for discovery beyond current edits. Invite 'none/not sure'; never steer users to claim a desired answer. At most three should be essential now; additional useful questions can be optional in the UI. Missing evidence must not prevent a usable initial edit.

report.outcomes: clearerNow, needsConfirmation, stillUnresolved, optionalImprovements arrays. Keep explicitly preferred qualifications and optional enhancements ONLY in optionalImprovements, not mixed into stillUnresolved. Label alternatives accurately; absence of one named alternative does not negate a demonstrated acceptable alternative. Explain what the edits clarify, which evidence needs answers, and what qualifications remain unestablished. Never imply rewriting closes missing professional tenure. No speculative post-edit score.

report.cleanup: distinct remaining issues not already addressed by edits, with text,severity (high|medium|low),sourceQuote (exact excerpt or empty when the observation is absence). No duplicate advice. Serious unfixable concerns must remain visible. Sensitive personal details can be removed, never added.

report.interviewPreparation: a short optional list of concrete prompts tied to the supported work the candidate should be able to explain. Do not invent interview stories or request internal documents.

Do not discard valuable evidence just because its project attribution needs clarification. Removing duplication is different from deleting unique accomplishments.
